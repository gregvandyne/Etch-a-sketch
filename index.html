The methodology

A six-phase operating system, delivered in 90 days.

Each phase produces a tangible output. Each output stress-tests the next phase. By the end, you own the system, not a deck about the system.

Phase 01

Discover

We interview your customers, your champions, your blockers, and your churn risks. We map what they actually use, what they say they want, and where the gap lives.

Phase 02

Map

We segment your base by ICP fit, deployment maturity, and expansion potential. Each segment gets a journey, success criteria, and a defined motion.

Phase 03

Instrument

We build a health scoring framework that predicts renewal, not one that decorates a dashboard. We configure your CRM to surface signal, not noise.

Phase 04

Activate

We design and document the lifecycle plays: onboarding, adoption milestones, expansion triggers, renewal motion. Each play has owners, timing, and outputs.

Phase 05

Operate

We run the motion alongside your team for the first cycle. Real customers, real renewals, real expansion conversations. The playbook gets stress-tested in production.

Phase 06

Compound

We hand off a documented operating system, a trained team, and a set of metrics tied to NRR. Six months later, the system is still running without us.
